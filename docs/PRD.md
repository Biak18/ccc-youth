# Christian City Church — Youth Website
## Product Requirements Document

## 1. Overview

A youth-focused website for Christian City Church, Yangon, Myanmar.

The website is the organized online home for the church's Youth ministry. It complements the church's existing Facebook presence by providing a permanent, searchable place for:

- Upcoming events
- Youth activities
- Photos
- Videos
- Announcements
- Youth leadership information
- Church and contact information
- Historical activity memories

The website should feel like a real Youth community rather than a generic corporate church website.

---

## 2. Brand

Existing brand assets are available:

- Church logo
- Youth group photograph / background image

Primary visual direction:

- Deep navy blue
- Red
- White
- Light blue accents

The existing church identity should remain recognizable throughout the site.

---

## 3. Goals

### Primary Goals

1. Give the Youth ministry a dedicated online presence.
2. Make upcoming events easy to discover.
3. Make past activities easy to browse.
4. Preserve photos and videos as a long-term Youth archive.
5. Allow authorized Youth leaders to publish and manage content.
6. Make the website useful on mobile devices.
7. Connect visitors to the church's existing Facebook page.

### Secondary Goals

- Encourage participation in Youth activities.
- Provide clear church and Youth information.
- Create a historical archive that becomes more valuable over time.
- Reduce dependence on Facebook as the only place where activity information exists.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Public Visitors

Visitors can:

- View the homepage.
- View upcoming events.
- Browse activities.
- View photos and videos.
- Read announcements.
- Learn about Youth leaders.
- View church information.
- Open the church's social media links.

Authentication is not required.

### Youth Members

Youth members can:

- Check upcoming events.
- Read announcements.
- Browse recent activities.
- View activity photos and videos.
- Explore previous years and memories.

### Youth Leaders

Authorized leaders can:

- Create activities.
- Create events.
- Upload photos.
- Add videos.
- Publish announcements.
- Save drafts.
- Edit their permitted content.

### Administrators

Administrators can:

- Manage all content.
- Manage Youth leaders.
- Publish or unpublish content.
- Delete content.
- Manage website settings.

---

## 5. Core Content Model

The website should distinguish between four major content types.

### Events

Answer:

> What's coming?

Examples:

- Youth Fellowship
- Bible Study
- Youth Conference
- Youth Camp
- Sports Day

### Activities

Answer:

> What happened?

Activities are the main historical content type and can contain:

- Activity date
- Description
- Location
- Cover image
- Multiple photos
- Multiple videos

### Announcements

Answer:

> What do people need to know?

Examples:

- Schedule changes
- Registration notices
- Meeting cancellations
- Important reminders

### Memories

Memories are primarily a presentation concept built from historical activities.

They should make the Youth archive feel personal and community-focused.

---

## 6. Homepage Requirements

The homepage should contain:

1. Hero
2. Next / Upcoming Event
3. Latest Activities
4. Latest Videos
5. Youth Memories
6. Announcements
7. Youth Leaders
8. Facebook / Social CTA
9. Footer

### Hero

Use the real Youth group photograph as the primary hero image.

Display:

- Church logo
- Christian City Church
- Youth
- Short tagline
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

Suggested tagline:

> Growing Together in Faith & Fellowship

Suggested CTAs:

- Upcoming Events
- Explore Activities

A dark overlay should be used over the photograph where needed to preserve text readability.

---

## 7. Navigation

Recommended public navigation:

- Home
- About
- Events
- Activities
- Memories
- Announcements
- Gallery
- Contact

On mobile, use a compact navigation menu.

---

## 8. Responsive Design

Mobile is a primary platform.

The website must work well on:

- Phones
- Tablets
- Laptops
- Desktop monitors

Important actions should remain easy to access on small screens.

---

## 9. Content Rules

Only published content should be visible publicly.

Draft content must never appear on the public website.

Every activity must preserve its actual activity date separately from its upload date.
